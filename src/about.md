Weekend Craft Lab is an independent hobby blog for people who want to make something with their hands but don't have a studio, a big budget or a free month. Every project is designed to fit into a weekend, often a single afternoon, using tools you can find at a hardware store, a craft chain or a cheap shop.

It's written in Brisbane, so you'll find notes about things that matter here: glue and paint that behave differently in humidity, resin that cures faster in the heat, and summer school holidays when kids want something to do.

## What to expect

- **Beginner-friendly tutorials** with a clear materials list and realistic timing.
- **Several methods for the same project** where it helps, so you can pick based on tools and budget.
- **Honest trade-offs.** If a technique is fiddly or a shortcut doesn't last, I'll say so.
- **Safety notes** for anything involving heat, blades, resin or solvents. Always read and follow the instructions on your own products.

## Disclosure

This is a hobby blog, not a shop. I enjoy making things, and now and then I may feature something I make myself. When that happens, I'll say so clearly in the post. There are no sponsored posts or paid product placements.
